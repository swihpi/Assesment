# Annex: personal context supplied by Stefan

The OCI Think Tank was an informal internal initiative that began with Stefan and Mark, an Oracle US sales representative. They brought in like-minded colleagues from EMEA and the US who shared an enthusiasm for experimenting with customer solutions that could generate Oracle revenue while creating commercial advantage and revenue opportunities for customers. Stefan contributed to architecture and tool considerations involving Oracle Integration Cloud / Data Integration, Autonomous Database, recovery considerations, and the flow into OAC, where he created a number of dashboards. A colleague configured the database sentiment analysis.

The original initiative focused on NHL hockey, including a Nashville Predators versus LA Kings illustration, and a broader landscape of business and social sources. The assessment extends that experience into a hypothetical sports organisation and CRM integration problem.

The wider thinking was to combine audience-level social sentiment with customer information held in other systems, supporting useful audience and commercial decisions. This was an internal initiative with an intended pilot through the OCI Sports Unit, rather than a customer request. The project was discontinued because of other project priorities within OCI OAC Product Management.

It was a learning experience bringing engineers, product management, and DBAs together around an idea intended to drive OCI usage revenue while helping customers derive commercial benefit from data. The CRM export problem, synthetic records, and processing rules in this assessment are hypothetical extensions, not incidents claimed from the original project.

The original presentation is internal source material used to check this account. The repository contains no copy of it or its screenshots. The project-status explanation and individual responsibilities remain Stefan's personal account.
